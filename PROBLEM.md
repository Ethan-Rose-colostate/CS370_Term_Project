CS370 Project – Ethan Rose, Evan Holland
1.	Our users are people who own plants and have issues with watering and light levels.
2.	Plants are often placed on windowsills where they are forgotten and dry out and get burnt by the sun. Eventually they are found and users may then drown in too much water and place then with too little sun to try to save them. Our product will Alert users too problematic moisture and sun levels. 
3.	Everyone brings their phone everywhere. No one wants to leave their phone sitting with their plant all day. Additionally, this device can be forgotten in the window with the plant and send notifications to ensure its watering and care. 
4.	We will use a light sensor and a moisture sensor. These work together to detect moisture levels and light levels.
5.	We picked to include D a custom storage layer to ensure we have enough storage for the rolling measurements. We picked E to help isolate failures especially with the risk of damaged components due to water.
6.	Water. Water is the most likely to sink this project as users will be watering a plant with this device. Water can easily fry the device or damage sensors.
